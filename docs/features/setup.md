# Setup, modules and your brand

## Setup checklist

A new workspace shows **Set up your workspace** at the top of the Overview, for the owner only. It lists what is left to do, how far you are ("5 of 9 done"), and a button that opens the exact place to fix each step:

| Step | Where it opens |
| --- | --- |
| Company details: name, registration number, address, email, phone | Settings > Company |
| Business logo | Settings > Documents |
| Bank details for transfers | Settings > Company |
| SST registration (optional) | Settings > Company |
| Email sending (SMTP) | Settings > Integrations |
| Online payments (optional) | Settings > Payments |
| Encrypted backups with an off-site copy | Settings > Security |
| Two-step sign-in (MFA) | Settings > Security |
| First client and first invoice | Add client, or New invoice |

- Not registered for SST? Press **Not registered**. No payment gateway yet? Press **Not now**. Both can be undone under **Done**.
- **Hide** removes the checklist on every device. Bring it back with **Show again** under Settings > Modules.
- The checklist goes away by itself when every step is done.

## Modules

Settings > Modules has a switch for each optional part of the app. Turn off what you don't use and the menu gets shorter:

Sales pipeline · Proforma invoices · Sales and delivery orders · Recurring billing · Bank matching · Expenses, bills and reports · Tax and e-Invoice · Projects, timesheets and inventory · Client portal and feedback · Automation and assistant · Webhooks

- Turning a module off hides its menu entries, its panels on the Overview and its sections in Settings. That is all: your records stay, recurring plans and reminders keep running, and links you sent keep working.
- Turn it back on and everything is where you left it.
- The Overview, quotations and invoices, clients, services, payments, Settings and team are always on.
- Everything is on until you change it, including after an upgrade.
- Only the owner can change modules.

## Your brand

Once you enter your business name (Settings > Company) the sidebar and the sign-in page show it instead of Nawlogic. Upload a logo under Settings > Documents (or **Change logo** in the account menu) and it shows there too, and on every PDF (documents, statements, receipts, purchase orders, reports, the accountant pack, SST-02 and self-billed e-Invoices) and at the top of emails. Remove it and everything goes back to the Nawlogic logo. Issued documents keep the logo they were issued with. The browser tab shows your business name as well.

The sign-in page can show your name and logo before anyone signs in, so those two things are public. Nothing else is: not your address, email, bank details or any other file.
